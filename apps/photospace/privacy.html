PhotoSpace

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

PhotoSpace ("the App") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoSpace accesses, processes, and handles information when you use the App on supported Apple devices.

PhotoSpace allows users to browse photos and videos stored on an iPhone or Mac through a web browser over a local network.

PhotoSpace is designed to operate without uploading your personal media to developer-operated cloud servers.

2. Photos and Videos

2.1 Photo Library Access on iPhone

PhotoSpace requests permission to access your photo library to provide its core functionality.

With your permission, PhotoSpace can:

  • Access photos and videos authorized by you.
  • Display available photo albums.
  • Generate thumbnails and media previews.
  • Make photos and videos available through a local web browser.
  • Allow original media files to be accessed or downloaded.

PhotoSpace only accesses media within the permissions granted by your device's operating system.

Your photos and videos are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer of PhotoSpace.

Original media files remain in your photo library unless you modify or delete them independently.

2.2 File Access on Mac

On macOS, PhotoSpace allows you to select folders containing photos and videos.

The App accesses the folders you explicitly authorize.

This access is used to identify supported media files and make them available through the local sharing service.

PhotoSpace does not require unrestricted access to your entire computer.

2.3 Browser Access

When you start the local sharing service, other devices that can connect to the service may access shared media through a web browser.

Media is transferred directly between the device running PhotoSpace and the accessing device over the local network.

Photos and videos downloaded through the browser may be stored on the accessing device.

PhotoSpace does not control how downloaded files are subsequently stored, copied, or shared.

3. Local Network Information

PhotoSpace uses local network communication to provide its core functionality.

The App may process local network information, such as:

  • Local IP addresses.
  • Network connection details.
  • Local sharing service status.

This information is used to establish and maintain connections between devices.

PhotoSpace does not transmit your personal media to developer-operated servers.

Local network communication may use HTTP, which does not provide encryption by itself.

We recommend using PhotoSpace only on trusted private networks.

4. Data Collection

PhotoSpace does not require users to create an account or provide personal registration information.

The App does not collect or store your photos and videos on developer-operated servers.

PhotoSpace does not use your personal media for advertising, profiling, or AI model training.

PhotoSpace does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information necessary for the App to function may be processed and stored locally on your device.

This may include application preferences, selected folder references, and temporary media data.

5. Local Data Storage

PhotoSpace may store certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Application preferences.
  • Selected folder references on Mac.
  • Local sharing settings.
  • Temporary thumbnails or cached media.

This information is used to support the App's functionality.

PhotoSpace does not maintain a cloud-based copy of your photo library.

Uninstalling PhotoSpace does not delete your original photos or videos.

Files previously downloaded to another device are not automatically deleted when you stop sharing or uninstall the App.

6. Third-Party Services

PhotoSpace uses Apple operating system technologies to access photos, files, and local network functionality.

PhotoSpace does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

The App does not require third-party cloud storage services for its core functionality.

Apple's operating system and platform services are subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

7. Permissions and User Control

PhotoSpace may request permissions required for its functionality.

On iPhone, you can grant, limit, or revoke photo library access through your device's system settings.

On Mac, you can select which folders PhotoSpace is allowed to access.

You can start or stop the local sharing service within the App.

Disabling permissions may prevent certain features from working.

8. Data Security

PhotoSpace is designed to provide local media access without requiring cloud-based media storage.

However, local network communication does not guarantee complete security.

Devices that can reach the local sharing service may be able to request shared content.

If the service uses HTTP, information transmitted over the local network is not encrypted by HTTP itself.

We recommend:

  • Using trusted private networks.
  • Avoiding public or untrusted Wi-Fi networks.
  • Stopping the sharing service when it is not needed.
  • Keeping your devices and operating systems updated.

PhotoSpace cannot guarantee the security of files after they have been downloaded to another device.

9. Children's Privacy

PhotoSpace is a general-purpose photo and video browsing utility.

The App is not specifically directed toward children.

PhotoSpace does not require users to submit personal registration information.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of devices and shared media.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can control access to your media by:

  • Managing photo library permissions.
  • Selecting or removing shared folders on Mac.
  • Starting or stopping the local sharing service.
  • Removing the App from your device.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights concerning personal information.

For privacy-related questions, please contact us using the information below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our data practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date will be revised.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
